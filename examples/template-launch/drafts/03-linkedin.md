## Post

I have published a free template for people who have a software idea and no technical co-founder.
I made it myself. It is MIT licensed, and nothing is for sale.

The aim is to build a software startup without writing code, and it is deliberately not vibe
coding. Vibe coding is describing what you want to an AI, accepting whatever it writes, and asking
again when it breaks. This template makes AI agents work the way an established software team
does: requirements are written and numbered before anything is built, a spec has to be confirmed
before work starts, someone other than the builder tests the work and can stop a release, and
nothing goes live, costs money or deletes data without the founder's approval. Every step in the
README is paired with its standard name, so you can look it up.

You play the founder and the final approver. The agents play the team.

Where it stands, honestly: the setup interview has a recorded test run, played by an AI in the
role of a founder, and it passed in Claude Code. A run on another tool failed and led to fixes. No
real non-technical founder has used it yet, and I have not confirmed that Codex, Gemini CLI and
Cursor read the files as intended. The agents still make mistakes, and the legal agent writes
checklists and first drafts for a lawyer to review, not legal advice. You also need a paid
subscription to an AI coding tool.

If you have an idea and no developer, the README walks through the steps. If you work with
software teams, I would value hearing what a real team would insist on that is missing.
