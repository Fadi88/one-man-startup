## Title

I made a free template that gives you an AI agent "team" for building a software product, following the normal software process

## Body

I made this and it is free (MIT licensed, nothing to sell).

If you have a side project idea but are not a developer, the usual advice is to ask an AI to
build it. That works for a demo, and tends to end in something that half works and that nobody
understands. This template tries the other way: AI agents take the roles of a normal team, and
the process is the normal one. You are interviewed first, the work is written down as numbered
requirements, someone other than the builder tests it, and nothing goes live or costs money
without your approval.

You use it by clicking "Use this template" on GitHub, opening your copy in an AI coding tool, and
running the setup interview. The README walks through it. It is written for Claude Code, Codex,
Gemini CLI and Cursor, and you need a paid subscription to one of them.

Honest limits: the setup interview has one recorded test run with an AI playing the founder, and
no real non-technical person has tried it yet. I have not confirmed that Codex, Gemini CLI or
Cursor read the files the way I expect. The agents still make mistakes, and the legal agent is not
legal advice.
