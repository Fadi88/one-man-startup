# The decision card

Every decision the founder is asked for, in chat or in `p1-open-questions.md`, is one card
(TPL-113), at their level:

```
**Decision:** put the new sign-up page live for your users.
**Recommendation:** yes. It passed every check and the old page is kept.
**Could go wrong:** people who already started signing up may have to start again.
**Undo:** I put the old page back, about two minutes.
**Reply with one:**
`yes, put it live`
`no, keep the old page for now`
```

Two replies, the recommended one first, each short enough to copy and send as it is. A step
that spends money, deletes something or messages customers says so on the card, and a step
that cannot be undone says "cannot be undone" on the Undo line.

## The same step at each level

- `non-technical`: "Ready to put the new sign-up page live. If anything looks wrong, I can put
  the old one back in about two minutes. Go ahead?"
- `basics`: "Ready to deploy the new sign-up page. Rollback is about two minutes. Go?"
- `technical`: "Deploy v1.4 to production? Rollback is redeploying v1.3, about two minutes."
